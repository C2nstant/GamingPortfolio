About Me:

I am Earl Jaissen M. Dizon but I go by my IGN (In Game Name) which is “Constant”. I have been playing video games since I was in grade school. The first games I played are from the PS2 Console such as Ghostbusters (which was more like a horror game to me at that time) and Champions: Return to Arms. After that, I got into mobile gaming where I used to play from my mother's work phone.

My first ever pc game was Minecraft (Version 1.7) which was gifted to me for my 8th birthday and is probably my most played game to date. It took me till I was 15 years old to start buying my own games from the small amount of money I'm saving from my allowance. The first two games I bought and finished to completion were Hollow Knight and Enter the Gungeon which are still some of my favorites till now.

Since I primarily played singleplayer games, It was only 3 years ago that I played my first FPS (First Person Shooter) game which was Valorant. It first started as just playing a few times a month with my friends, but now I have over 850 hours of playtime!

As you can probably guess from this website, I'm a game completionist, a style of playing where one tries to experience everything the game has in store for them before moving on to the next game. My style is a bit different from the definition though, I play games till I have done everything possible in them or if I don't enjoy the game anymore, I drop it entirely.

Currently, in my completionist journey, I am playing the PC games Slay the Spire, Disco Elysium, Hades, and Outer Wilds. On mobile, I am playing a roguelite game called Balatro (Which I highly recommend!).
